# Privacy Policy: Crocs Visualizer

**Effective Date:** 2026-11-04  
**Version:** 1.0.0-beta  

---

## 1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We believe in radical user privacy. **Crocs Visualizer does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).**

- **No User Accounts:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to use the application.
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not embed behavioral trackers, session recorders, or advertising pixels.
- **No Cookies:** The application does not use server-side tracking cookies.

---

## 2. Client-Side Local Storage

The Application uses your web browser's native `localStorage` feature solely to save your customized shoe configurations (such as selected color, size, and placed charm positions) directly on your device.

- **Device Boundary:** Your saved designs remain exclusively within your browser and are never transmitted to our servers or third parties.
- **Data Erasure:** You can clear your saved configurations at any time by clicking "Reset Canvas" in the app or clearing your browser's site data/cookies.

---

## 3. Contact

For privacy or legal questions regarding this project, please open an issue in the official project repository.
